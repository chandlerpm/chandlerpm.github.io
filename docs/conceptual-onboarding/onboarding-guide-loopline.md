## Welcome to Loopline

**Last updated:** 2026-07 · **Audience:** New users · **Reading time:** ~5 minutes

---

Welcome to Loopline — a project workflow and task management platform designed to keep your team aligned, your work visible, and your projects moving forward.

This guide will help you get oriented quickly. By the end, you'll know where to find your work, how to communicate with your team, and where to go when you have questions.

---

### What Is Loopline?

Loopline organizes work into three core layers:

| Layer | What It Is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Your organization's top-level container | Acme Corp |
| **Project** | A collection of related work | Q3 Product Launch |
| **Task** | A single unit of work with an owner and due date | Write release notes |

Everything in Loopline lives inside a Workspace. You'll be invited to specific Projects within that Workspace depending on your role.

---

### Your First Login

When you receive your invitation email:

1. Click **Accept Invitation** in the email.
2. Create your password (minimum 12 characters).
3. Set your display name and time zone — this affects due date displays and notifications.
4. You'll land on your **My Work** dashboard, which shows all tasks currently assigned to you.

> **Tip:** Set your time zone correctly before you do anything else. Loopline schedules notifications and deadline reminders based on this setting.

---

### Navigating the Interface

The left sidebar is your main navigation:

- **My Work** — your personal task view across all projects
- **Inbox** — mentions, comments, and notifications
- **Projects** — all projects you have access to
- **Docs** — shared documents and wikis attached to projects
- **Settings** — your profile, notifications, and integrations

## First-Week Onboarding Checklist

**Audience:** New Loopline users · **Complete within:** Your first 5 business days

---

Use this checklist to make sure you're fully set up and ready to collaborate. Each item links to additional documentation if you need it.

---

### Day 1 — Get Set Up

- [ ] Accept your invitation email and log in for the first time
- [ ] Set your display name and time zone in **Settings → Profile**
- [ ] Upload a profile photo (helps teammates identify you in comments)
- [ ] Review your **My Work** dashboard — confirm your assigned tasks look correct
- [ ] Install the Loopline desktop app or bookmark the web app

---

### Day 2 — Explore Your Projects

- [ ] Navigate to **Projects** in the left sidebar
- [ ] Open each project you've been added to and review the project overview
- [ ] Familiarize yourself with your team's task naming conventions (ask your team lead if unclear)
- [ ] Check due dates on your assigned tasks and flag anything that needs clarification

---

### Day 3 — Communication & Notifications

- [ ] Review your **Inbox** and clear any pending notifications
- [ ] Configure notification preferences in **Settings → Notifications**
  - Recommended: enable email digests, disable real-time email for comments
- [ ] Leave a comment on at least one task to confirm your @mentions are working
- [ ] Connect Loopline to Slack or Microsoft Teams if your organization uses it (**Settings → Integrations**)

---

### Day 4 — Docs & Knowledge Base

- [ ] Navigate to **Docs** within one of your projects
- [ ] Read any existing project documentation your team has created
- [ ] Locate your team's style guide or documentation standards (ask your team lead if none exists)

---

### Day 5 — Confirm & Check In

- [ ] Verify all your tasks have accurate due dates and are assigned correctly
- [ ] Complete or update the status on at least one task
- [ ] Check in with your team lead — confirm you have access to everything you need
- [ ] Bookmark the [Help Center](https://help.loopline.io) for future reference

---

### You're All Set

Once you've completed this checklist, you're ready to work in Loopline. For a deeper look at product features, see the [Feature Orientation Guide](#feature-orientation-guide).

## Feature Orientation Guide

**Audience:** New Loopline users · **Last updated:** 2025-01

---

This guide provides a plain-language overview of Loopline's core features. It's designed to help you understand what each feature does and when to use it — not to replace the full Help Center documentation.

---

### Tasks

Tasks are the basic unit of work in Loopline. Every task has:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Title** | A clear, action-oriented description of the work |
| **Assignee** | The person responsible for completing it |
| **Due date** | The target completion date |
| **Status** | Current state: To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done |
| **Priority** | Low, Medium, High, or Urgent |
| **Description** | Context, requirements, and relevant links |

**Best practice:** Task titles should start with a verb and describe a specific outcome. "Write release notes for v2.4" is better than "Release notes."

---

### Projects

Projects group related tasks together. Each project has:

- **Overview** — a summary of the project's goals, owner, and timeline
- **Task list** — all tasks associated with the project, filterable by status, assignee, and due date
- **Timeline view** — a Gantt-style view of tasks and dependencies
- **Docs** — a shared space for project documentation, meeting notes, and references

---

### My Work

My Work is your personal dashboard. It aggregates all tasks assigned to you across every project into a single view. Use it to:

- See everything on your plate at a glance
- Sort and filter by due date or priority
- Quickly update task status without navigating into individual projects

---

### Inbox

Your Inbox collects all activity directed at you:

- @mentions in task comments
- Status changes on tasks you're watching
- Due date reminders
- Project invitations

Mark items as read or archive them once addressed to keep your Inbox clean.

---

### Docs

Docs is Loopline's built-in wiki and document editor. Use it to:

- Write and maintain project documentation
- Store meeting notes and decisions
- Create shared references your whole team can access

Docs are attached to specific projects and are visible to everyone with project access.

---

### Integrations

Loopline connects with the tools your team already uses. Supported integrations include Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Drive, GitHub, and Zapier. Configure integrations in **Settings → Integrations**.

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

**Audience:** Loopline users · **Last updated:** 2025-01

---

### Account & Access

**I didn't receive my invitation email.**
Check your spam or junk folder first. If it's not there, ask your account admin to resend the invitation from **Settings → Team → Members**.

**I can't log in after setting my password.**
Make sure you're using the email address your invitation was sent to — not an alias or alternate address. If you're still locked out, use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page.

**I don't see a project I'm supposed to have access to.**
Project access is managed by your account admin or project owner. Ask them to verify your membership in **Project Settings → Members**.

---

### Tasks

**A task is assigned to me but I can't find it.**
Navigate to **My Work** in the left sidebar. All tasks assigned to you across every project appear here. If it's still missing, confirm with the task owner that it's assigned to your correct email address.

**I accidentally changed a task's due date. Can I undo it?**
Yes. Open the task and click **Activity Log** in the right panel. You'll see a full history of changes. To restore a previous value, manually re-enter the correct date — Loopline does not currently support one-click undo for field edits.

**How do I mark a task complete?**
Click the status dropdown on the task and select **Done**. The task will move out of active views but remains searchable and accessible in project history.

---

### Notifications

**I'm getting too many email notifications.**
Go to **Settings → Notifications** and adjust your email preferences. Most teams find the **Daily Digest** setting (one summary email per day) to be the most manageable.

**I'm not getting notifications I expect.**
Check that the relevant project or task is in your **Watching** list. You only receive notifications for tasks assigned to you or tasks you've explicitly chosen to watch.

---

### Docs

**I accidentally deleted content in a Doc.**
Loopline Docs saves version history automatically. Click the clock icon in the top right of any Doc to access previous versions and restore content.

**Who can see the Docs in a project?**
All members of a project can view and edit its Docs by default. Permissions can be restricted to view-only for specific members in **Project Settings → Permissions**.
