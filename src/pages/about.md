# Twenty-plus years in technical documentation

I'm a Senior Technical Writer and knowledge systems specialist with over twenty years of experience making complex information clear, accessible, and built to last. My work spans government defense, SaaS, enterprise operations, and higher education — different domains, always documentation as the throughline.

What I bring to a team is the ability to embed with engineering, product, and operations groups and build content systems that scale: developer and API documentation, SOPs, knowledge bases, onboarding programs, governance frameworks. The samples on this site span exactly that range — a CAD-team reference guide and onboarding series, freelance SaaS documentation, and several self-directed projects (an SOP library, a knowledge-base architecture framework, a documentation-tooling system) written to demonstrate the systems thinking behind the writing, not just the writing itself.

My degree is in engineering physics — the only technical degree I claim in this space, and probably why I gravitate toward documentation that has to be structurally correct as much as clearly written: specs, data models, API contracts, the kind of writing where an inaccuracy costs someone real time.

I work in Markdown and Git as a matter of course — docs-as-code isn't a special workflow for me, it's the default — and I bring project-management fluency to documentation work, which tends to mean things ship on schedule, with stakeholder buy-in, and structured to survive the next reorg or product pivot.

Every sample on this site links back to its source repository, so you can see the commit history and, where relevant, the code the documentation describes.

I'm based in the Providence, RI / Boston, MA metro area and open to full-time remote or hybrid roles.
